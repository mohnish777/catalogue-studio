# Asset attribution

- Product photography is bundled from the [Fake Store API repository](https://github.com/keikaavousi/fake-store-api/tree/master/public/img). The numbered files in `backend/public/images/` are used for the explicit demo dataset. Demo product titles/descriptions and ratings are illustrative; they do not assert real product availability or specifications.
- Instrument Serif and DM Sans come from the [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts). Their SIL Open Font License texts are included in `docs/licenses/`.
- The app mark and simple navigation/search/check glyphs are drawn locally with Android vector/Compose drawing primitives. No icon account or network font download is required.
